Round 3, on the finished first version. The reviewer adopted `.claude/agents/art-director.md` and played
the build headless; a skeptic then tried to reproduce and refute every finding. The owner decisions
are in `decisions.md`; DECISIONS.md cites these as "review-3 A<n>".

**Verdict:** Ship with fixes. In the running game the art holds together. The eight families read apart at 1x and at 3x (showroom on four floors), all 43 PixelLab items pass the envelope, closed-outline and 3:1 belt rules, every layer has its own look, Mags reads well, and the title looks as designed at exact 720p and 1080p. The Washer and the Separator have clearly different shapes beside each other (the Separator stays side-on, as M3 chose, so I don't re-raise it). The working Heat line glows and smokes in the right places. The Heart, framed at last, has two problems, filed as A12. What stops a sign-off is what players see most. On the Surface's first screen the wood crate stacks shout over the starter buildings, the floor is one tile repeated, and the mountain face is a mesh. The title breaks at window sizes between the standard ones. Every elevator station draws its pit and cab outside the art's pit. The ending never shows the Heart waking. ## A2 (major, Title screen at in-between window sizes)

**Problem.** drawTitleBackdrop (windows.ts:89) picks the cover scale in UI units. With the UI at scale 2 the real pixel scale can only be even, so any canvas just over 1280x720 jumps to 6x (1920x1080) and loses about 30% of the art's width and height. The logo (UI y 28) then lands on the Drill's derrick and yellow housing instead of the dark sky (#e8c170 text on #de9e41). The mountain's peak and the Heart are cropped away. Only exact 720p, 1080p, 1440p and 4K canvases look as designed, and a browser window on the commonest laptop screens is none of them.

**Evidence.** [seen] ...\art-director\shots\title_1366.png: 1366x768 drawn at 6x, 554x312 px cropped, 'THE' on the derrick, the subtitle across the drill, no Heart. ...\shots\title_1366x657.png and ...\shots\title_1536x730.png: browser windows on 1366x768 and 1536x864 screens, same break. ...\shots\title_1280.png: 4x, correct. Measured: the drill is at screen x 286-490, which is art x 94-128 at 6x with a -278 px offset. [code] windows.ts:89: s = ceil(max(ui.w/art.width, ui.h/art.height)) in UI units.

**Proposed fix.** Free (code, plus one STYLE line). In drawTitleBackdrop, compute the scale in screen pixels and fit instead of cover: const k = app.uiScale(); const S = Math.max(1, Math.floor(Math.min(ui.w * k / art.width, ui.h * k / art.height))). Draw the art at art.width * S / k by art.height * S / k UI units, centred. Fill the margins by stretching the art's outermost 1 px row or column into them (navy sky at the top, brick at the bottom), so there are no bars. Return the art's top and S, and draw the logo at art y 14 and the subtitle at art y 25 (y = artTop + 14 * S / k in UI units). These reproduce today's exact positions at 720p and 1080p and keep the logo on the sky band at every size. Change STYLE's title row from 'smallest whole-number scale that covers' to 'largest that fits, edges extended'.

**Check: real.** windows.ts:89 computes s = ceil(max(ui.w/320, ui.h/180)) in UI units, and uiScale() = round(canvasH/330). On my server at 1366x768 (k=2, ui 683x384) the art draws at 6x. **Notes on the fix.** Fit is the right direction, with corrections. (1) Don't stretch row 0. It holds one #577277 star pixel (319 #172038 + 1), which becomes a vertical streak through the whole top margin. The bottom row stretches into vertical brick stripes. Mock: <session scratchpad>\review3\art-skeptic\a2_fit_1440x900.png (the streak is at x~213). Fill the top margin with #172038 and the bottom with #151d28 (the last row's commonest colour). Draw the backdrop under setTransform(1,0,0,1,0,0) with ox=floor((W-320S)/2) and oy=floor((H-180S)/2): at k=3 the UI-unit sizes are fractional (1280/3). (3) Round the text. (4) Update scripts/art/title_mock.ts, which copies the ceil(max) rule at line 69, and STYLE's 'Title art' row. Expect large margins at some sizes: 1440x900 gets S=4 with 80/90 px margins.
