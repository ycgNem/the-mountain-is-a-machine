# Review 3: indie critic

Round 3, on the finished first version. The reviewer adopted `.claude/agents/indie-critic.md` and played
the build headless; a skeptic then tried to reproduce and refute every finding. The owner decisions
are in `decisions.md`; DECISIONS.md cites these as "review-3 K<n>".

**Verdict:** Ship with fixes. The game plays end to end, and the first 15 minutes work. From the title it took one click, the first sale landed in under 10 s, my first building went down at 0:21, and every Act I step was clear from Mags and the objective panel. A deliberately sloppy run (a reversed belt, a merged line, a power overload, a half-fed Assembler) still repaired the Drill at 17:06; the bot takes 12:45 and the band is 15–25. The loop at every timescale is "Mags names a goal, place 1–3 machines and belts, watch counters for 1–4 minutes". It breaks only where direction or exits can't be read. The elevator and requests now read clearly. The top station shows "Down 45/45 a min (requests 45, belts 0)" in amber with "Requested below: 19 items", the Stack shows "Mk1 45/m", and depot rows read as stock/target. The ghost tooltips are the best thing in the build: "Iron Ore (rich): 60/min", "Would run at 74% (spores)", "Must touch water", "Needs steady ground: 8 tiles from any hot ground". So is Mags's starve advice ("Make some and belt it into the HQ, or Rush the plan"). Pillar 1 check: two materials force new factory decisions. Emberite does, through a real hot line that runs an insulated belt to steady ground and three routes sharing 90/min of vents; I built it and it read well. Chorus Crystal does too, because 15/min vents force the rich route. Thrumstone is a placement-and-sequence rule more than an allocation choice, but that suits a finale. Sapstone's wash-or-separate is still optional; it was kept in #100, and my only new evidence is that M13 is met with Separators alone. What holds the game back is four things. The first transition sends players to a station face that silently refuses cargo (K1). Facing and belt direction can't be read once built (K2). The build bar eats a quarter of the screen from Act III (K4). The finale's payoff is mostly off-screen (K3, K5). Must fix before release: K1, K2, K4, K6. Should fix: K3, K5. What the game most needs next is its payoff: an ending that actually shows the mountain waking (K3+K5). After that it needs one chained playthrough of Acts II–VI by a person, with no stand-ins. Every Act III–VI time and the Act VI wait come from stand-in supply, HANDOFF still records the chained Act IV bot stalling at 163/360, and only that run will show whether old lines starving deeper requests is a quit point. No console errors or warnings appeared in any session. I stopped my servers on 5211 and 5312. All screenshots are in <session scratchpad>\review3\indie-critic\shots\. The new-game radio and toast transcript with game times is <session scratchpad>\review3\indie-critic\playlog_newgame.txt.

**Evidence.** [played] New game, D1 done at 17:06 (game time 1026 s). The Drill footprint (30-34,9-13) becomes a 3x3 station at (31-33,10-12), so every line that fed the Drill's south face at x=31..33 now points at the exit. The only clue is the station inspector's "Belt items into any side but the front", which the player has to open.

**Proposed fix.** (1) progress.ts:390: "...belt them into the top station's sides (its front is where arrivals come out), or into the HQ." (2) Add 'elevator' to JAM_KINDS (logistics.ts:573) with jamWhy 'front' when travel === opp(t.dir). Draw the ≠ badge on the station, and make its hover and inspector say "A belt is pushing into the exit: feed a side face." (3) Optional rule change: refuse only at the single output tile (front-centre), so the two front corners accept cargo and lines at x=31 and x=33 just work.

**Check: real.** Reproduced. In ?scenario=start I laid belts north on x=31 (y14-20) into the Drill's south face and finished D1 through progress.completeProject. Mags's line fired (progress.ts:390), and the top station stood at (31,10) facing dir 2 (south). I added a belt at (31,13) facing north and put Reinforced Plates on the line. After 20 s the plates sat at (31,13), (31,14) and (31,15) with prog 1, the station's jam was null and its status idle, and the bay was empty. Hovering the belt shows only 'Conveyor / Carrying Reinforced Plate'; hovering the station shows 'Freight Elevator / Click for details. R rotates.' In noteJam the else branch looks up recipesFor('elevator'), which is empty, so every side refusal (bay full, lower depot full, unstable item) would be marked 'wrong'. Otherwise keep skip. Add tests for both cases beside placement.test.ts:56-75, and amend #78/#54 and PLAN §5.1 ('Where it starts, ends or turns on a belt it leaves that belt alone').
